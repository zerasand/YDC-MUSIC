Artist Spotlight

Sand Zera

Composer · Producer

Musical direction, composition, modular synthesis, orchestration, and audio mastering. Championing authentic, high-emotion neoclassical and modular hybrid compositions that redefine cinematic tension and immersive audio narrative.I love making music from the ground up — from the first chord or melody to the arrangement, instrumentation, and final production. My work moves freely between original compositions, reinterpretations, and music made for games and other creative projects. I’m especially drawn to music that creates a feeling, builds an atmosphere, or makes a world feel more alive. I don't want to be tied to one genre — if the project calls for it, I'll make it.
